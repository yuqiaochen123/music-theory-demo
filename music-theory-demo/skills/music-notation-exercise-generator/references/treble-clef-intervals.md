# Treble-clef interval reference

Use this reference for Grade 5 major/minor-third exercise banks.

| Interval | Audio | Written spelling | Upper note position |
|---|---|---|---|
| Major third | G3–B3 | G–B | B space below the treble staff |
| Minor third | G3–B♭3 | G–B♭ | Same B space; flat before B |
| Major third | D4–F♯4 | D–F♯ | F line; sharp before F |
| Minor third | A3–C4 | A–C | C ledger line below the staff |
| Major third | C4–E4 | C–E | E bottom line |
| Minor third | C4–E♭4 | C–E♭ | E bottom line; flat before E |

For a third, the letter names must be two steps apart: C–E, D–F, E–G, F–A, G–B, A–C, or B–D. Determine the accidental only after the written letters are fixed.
