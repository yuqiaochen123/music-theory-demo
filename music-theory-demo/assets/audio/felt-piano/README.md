# Soft piano samples

These five `pp` Steinway Model B samples come from the University of Iowa
Musical Instrument Samples collection. The collection states that its
recordings may be downloaded and used in projects without restriction.

Source: https://theremin.music.uiowa.edu/MISpiano.html

The original AIFF recordings were converted locally to 96 kbps AAC/M4A for
fast browser playback. Nearby MIDI pitches are produced from the closest
chromatic anchor sample.
